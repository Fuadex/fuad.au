# THE LENS SWEEP — mandatory, and partitioned by LAYER rather than by work

You audit ONE lens field across the whole batch: every work's `see`, or every work's `about`, or every
`craft`, or every `context` — every paragraph of one kind in the wave, read together. ⚠ THE COUNT VARIES BY WAVE: five in wave 12, ten in wave 13, three in wave 14. Check the addendum at the end of this file for the wave you are on.

**Why it is partitioned this way.** An agent given four whole works fixes each one sensibly and cannot
see the batch, so four such agents independently converge on the same phrasing and drive the batch's
dominant tic UP while introducing errors. Reading one field across twenty works is the only way to see
both what the field gets wrong as a field and where the twenty are saying the same thing.

**Why it exists at all.** Three consecutive repair passes in an earlier wave swept the STOPS and never
once read the lenses; twelve of that wave's final twenty-six findings lived in the lenses. A later
wave's dedicated sweep found sixty-six lens findings across twenty-five tours, with exactly one tour
clean. The pattern is always the same: **a lens takes a stop's carefully scoped observation and
promotes it to a universal, and nobody re-reads it.**

## What to do

Read your field in EVERY `out_tour_*.json` file in this directory, then read each work's stops
to check the lens against them. You may open the plate `img_<id>.jpg` and the tiles when a claim needs
settling. ⛔ CORRECTED 2026-09-24: an earlier version said most of this work is textual and needs no image. A wave-14 `craft` sweep reported that FALSE for its field — BOTH of its BLOCKs required the plate. Open the plate whenever a claim is about the surface.

Check, per paragraph:

1. **Does the lens contradict its own stops?** When it does, **the lens is wrong** — the stops did the
   looking. Bring the lens into line with the stop's own wording rather than inventing a third
   phrasing; in seven findings of an earlier wave the true, properly scoped sentence was already
   written in a stop of the same tour.
2. **Scope creep.** A stop says "in this passage"; the lens says "everywhere". A stop counts what is
   in one crop; the lens counts the picture. Flag every promotion of a local observation.
3. **Absolutes and superlatives** — *no, never, only, nothing, not a single, the most, the largest,
   the brightest, the darkest*. Each must hold against the WHOLE plate. These are cheap to find and
   were never checked in the wave that paid for this rule.
4. **Finish claims** — *loose, dissolves, blurred, unfinished, barely painted, no contour*. The
   dissolution bias is directional: every wrong finish claim in three audits was wrong toward more
   dissolved.
5. **Law 2 — the lens spends a stop's instance.** `see` NAMES a thing; it may not describe it.
   `craft` names a METHOD; it may not spend the INSTANCE that a stop owns. Where both hold the same
   thing, **cut it from the lens, never from the stop** — the stop is where the reader is looking at it.
6. **Law 4 — does the sentence teach?** If a minute of unaided looking would give the reader the same
   thing, it is a caption. Say so, even where it is perfectly true.
7. **Hedge integrity.** A doubt in `flags` may not be asserted flat in a lens, and a hedge may not be
   cashed in by a later sentence in the same paragraph.
8. **The batch view — this is the part only you can do.** List every construction, opening shape,
   rhythm or ARGUMENT that recurs across your paragraphs. Name the works. An earlier wave
   produced ten works with ten distinct theses whose lenses all made ONE argument, and no gram gate
   could see it, because they shared a shape and not a phrase. Report the recurrence even where each
   instance is individually fine; one licensed use per batch is the rule, and the orchestrator decides
   which one keeps it.

## How to report
Write `lenssweep_<field>.md`. Per finding: the work id, the field, the sentence, what is wrong, and
the evidence. Grade BLOCK / SHOULD / MINOR as the crop brief does. End with a CHECKED, NONE FOUND list
naming every class above you checked clean, and a separate BATCH GAIT section for item 8.

⛔ You are read-only: report, never edit. Your diagnosis and your proposed wording have different
reliability — offer wording only where you are certain, marked as a suggestion. And a claim absent
from the tour is not thereby unsupported: some material is deliberately reserved for a later layer.
